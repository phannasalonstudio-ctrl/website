Meet
Phanna So.

Founder & Certified Advanced Hair Extension Specialist
Phanna So Salon Studio

With over 20 years in the beauty industry, I bring professional experience from California, Arizona, and Maryland to every appointment.

My passion is helping you look and feel your best by enhancing your natural beauty through personalized care, professional expertise, and high-quality beauty services.

My background includes hairstyling and makeup artistry in California, and high-end salon experience at Regis and Carlton Hair Salons in Arizona. These experiences shaped my approach: technical skill, creativity, and personal attention.
